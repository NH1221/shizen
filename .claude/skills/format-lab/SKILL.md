# Format lab (Shizen)
Run this before a concept gets a still card. A concept that fails here never reaches Flow.

## 1. Name the job and the mechanism
Pick ONE primary job and the mechanism behind it:
- Share (send to a friend): amusement or surprise with a clear payoff, awe at the craft, recognisable situations. Evidence A for news articles (Berger and Milkman 2012: high-arousal emotion and surprise raise sharing); not tested on short video.
- Save: practical utility (how to choose, size, wash, pack). Evidence A for sharing, B/C for saves.
- Comment: a question the viewer answers from their own life in 3 words, or an argument they can take a side in. Evidence B (votes and abstract design questions got 0 comments).
- Watch time: a story or list that pays off only at the end (open loop). Evidence: TikTok 2020 newsroom (historical) says finishing a longer video is a strong signal; B (Part 2, 33% full watch, one run).
- Search: on-screen text and caption that match real queries ("best no gi rash guard", "rash guard bjj"). Evidence B (Part 2 search tail, one post) + C (CHILLOLOG 86% search, one case).

## 2. Structural novelty gate (the P14 lesson)
List the last 3 posts on these axes; the new concept must differ on at least TWO:
1. Shot count and structure (one still / two-shot before-after / sequence)
2. Narrative shape (pun reveal / story with consequence / list / question / instruction)
3. Camera relationship (the real picture, never the text): subject's back to a static camera / subject turns to face the camera / subject moves toward or away from the camera / subject's posture changes in frame (standing to down) / two shots cut together. Pronouns in the text are NOT a structural axis.
4. Content job (joke / utility / identity / craft)
5. Length band (under 6 s / 7 to 12 s / 13 to 20 s)
6. Audio role (music drop / ambient / text-paced)
Changing only the wording, design, gym, caption, CTA or a POV/job LABEL is NOT a new format. A single calm back shot with text and a print payoff is the OLD format, whatever the words say; if used, label it the control.

## 2b. Adapted checks (sources in references/community-skills-audit.md)
- Frame-0 motion (HARD gate for every new format): something visibly moves within the first second (a body lowering, a head turn starting, a walk, heavy breathing large enough to read on a phone), and the picture shows what the words say. No exemptions; the old-format control is the only post allowed a still opening.
- Visible state change or reversal (provisional, from three sampled viral BJJ shorts, see pipeline/research/2026-11-19-instinct-visual-notes.md): the payoff is something the viewer SEES change (upright to flattened, intimidating to sheepish), not only a line of text or a print reveal. Hypothesis, not proven; never fake spectacle the model cannot render in low motion.
- Stranger test: someone who neither trains nor knows Shizen gets it from frame 1.
- Open loop: the payoff lands only in the last second; the hook cannot be resolved without it.
- BUT/THEREFORE beats, last line written first, last line hands back into the first for the loop.
- No fabricated biography: no first-person stories presented as the account's real life; fictional third-person characters only.
- Backup per slot: name a backup concept on the same base that fits the remaining submits; never post a failed take to fill a day.

## 3. Evidence line
Write: mechanism, grade (A platform or peer-reviewed; B our data; C anecdote), and what is hypothesis.

## 4. Budget fit
Submits needed (each still and each clip is 1; max 4 per day, 20 min apart; low motion only; real unused base; every person replaced). If it needs more than 4, redesign.

## 5. Decision metrics (age-matched)
Read at 4 h and 23 h after posting. Compare with earlier posts read at the SAME age where those reads exist (lifetime totals of older posts are not a matched control):
- full watch %, average watch as % of length, % at 1 s, views
- engagement per 1,000 views: comments, shares, saves (separately), likes
State the pass line and the FALSIFIER before posting ("the format is dropped if..."). With ~700 views, rates built on 1 to 3 events are fragile: a pass is provisional, two passes are a stronger hint, not proof.

## 6. Hard limits (never trade off)
Real unused base, all identities replaced, exact art, adult mainly male cast, no tiger/Tsuru, no product claims (including implied construction or quality claims about Shizen) unless independently sourced, Nic hands-off, manual AI label off with the stop rule, stop on any integrity flag.
