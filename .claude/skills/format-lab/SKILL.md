# Format lab (Shizen)
Run this before a concept gets a still card. A concept that fails here never reaches Flow.

## 1. Name the job and the mechanism
Pick ONE primary job and the mechanism behind it:
- Share (send to a friend): relatability that names a person type ("the guy who..."), amusement or surprise with a clear payoff, awe at the craft. Evidence A (Berger and Milkman: high-arousal emotion and surprise raise sharing).
- Save: practical utility (how to choose, size, wash, pack). Evidence A for sharing, B/C for saves.
- Comment: a question the viewer answers from their own life in 3 words, or an argument they can take a side in. Evidence B (votes and abstract design questions got 0 comments).
- Watch time: a story or list that pays off only at the end (open loop). Evidence A (finishing is a strong signal) + B (Part 2 33% full watch).
- Search: on-screen text and caption that match real queries ("best no gi rash guard", "rash guard bjj"). Evidence B (Part 2 search tail) + C (CHILLOLOG 86% search).

## 2. Structural novelty gate (the P14 lesson)
List the last 3 posts on these axes; the new concept must differ on at least TWO:
1. Shot count and structure (one still / two-shot before-after / sequence)
2. Narrative shape (pun reveal / story with consequence / list / question / instruction)
3. Point of view (third person about him / second person "you" / first person "me")
4. Content job (joke / utility / identity / craft)
5. Length band (under 6 s / 7 to 12 s / 13 to 20 s)
6. Audio role (music drop / ambient / text-paced)
Changing only the wording, design or gym is NOT a new format.

## 3. Evidence line
Write: mechanism, grade (A platform or peer-reviewed; B our data; C anecdote), and what is hypothesis.

## 4. Budget fit
Submits needed (each still and each clip is 1; max 4 per day, 20 min apart; low motion only; real unused base; every person replaced). If it needs more than 4, redesign.

## 5. Decision metrics (age-matched)
Read at 4 h and 23 h after posting, against the baseline in pipeline/STRATEGY-v2.md:
- full watch %, average watch as % of length, % at 1 s, views
- engagement per 1,000 views: comments, shares, saves (separately), likes
State the pass line and the FALSIFIER before posting ("the format is dropped if...").

## 6. Hard limits (never trade off)
Real unused base, all identities replaced, exact art, adult mainly male cast, no tiger/Tsuru, no product claims, Nic hands-off, manual AI label off with the stop rule, stop on any integrity flag.
