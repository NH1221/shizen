# Shizen strategy v2.2 (19 Nov 2026, final pass): format-first test plan with a ranked idea rerun
Built from pipeline/research/2026-11-19-virality-research.md and the format-lab skill (with checks adapted from four MIT community skill repos, see .claude/skills/format-lab/references/community-skills-audit.md). TikTok only (Instagram paused). Hands-off for Nic. Every post: unused real base, new identities, exact art, low motion, max 4 Flow submits a day, 20 min apart.

## What genuinely differs from the previous plan
- Previous plan: right goals, but every concept was produced as one near-still back, two text beats and a print reveal, because the 1 still + 1 clip budget quietly chose the format. P14 proved it.
- v2.1 designs the format first and requires, before any still: motion in the first second, an open loop paid off only at the end, a stranger test, and a structural difference from the last three posts on at least two axes (shot count, narrative shape, camera perspective, content job, length band, audio role). Wording, design, gym or CTA changes do not count.
- Every slot carries a backup concept and a hold rule; a failed take is never posted to fill a day.
- Results are read at the same ages (4 h, 23 h) as earlier reads where those exist. Lifetime totals of older posts are context, not a matched control.

## Provisional mechanisms from sampled viral BJJ shorts (Instinct, 19 Nov 12:38)
Three public shorts sampled by frame (ONE Championship "Just BJJ things"; FitFixx/@jdawgbjj "He Didn't See That Coming"; Moments Media judo vs jiu-jitsu); notes and frames in pipeline/research/2026-11-19-instinct-visual-notes.md. View totals are search snapshots, not verified; no audio or full shot breakdown; selected for being huge, from established channels with real footage, so heavily confounded. Observed in all three: a recognisable grappling behaviour, a concrete VISIBLE change of state, tension between two expectations, and captions that point at the action. Used here as hypotheses only, scaled down to what low-motion AI clips can show (posture and expression changes, not athletic action). None of their footage or choreography is reused.

### Why rank 1 wins
- It is the only idea whose payoff is a big visible state change between two shots (confident vs flattened), the mechanism seen in all three sampled shorts, scaled to what we can render (walking and lying still, both low motion).
- It is our own best-held structure (Part 2: a two-shot humbled-white-belt story, 33% full watch, one run, plus our only genuine comment).
- The print is present in both shots but is not the payoff, which breaks the P12 to P14 template on four axes (shot count, frame-0 motion, payoff type, camera relationship).
- Rank 2 is close and cheaper to source (one standing back shot), so it is the backup slot. Rank 3 has the strongest single-clip change but the highest motion risk. Ranks 4 and 5 put the payoff in text, which the sampled shorts did not rely on.

### Rank 1 spec: "one round" flash-forward
- Shot B (first on screen, 0 to ~1.3 s): the fictional man lying flat on his back on the mat after a round, chest heaving, arms out, staring at the ceiling. Text: "how it ended".
- Shot A (~1.3 s to ~6 s): the same fictional man walking onto the mat away from the camera, shoulders back, print visible. Text: "how it started" then, as he reaches the mat, "\"i'll just do one light round\"".
- Last ~1 s: cut back to shot B; he lets out one long breath. Loops into frame 1.
- Caption: one light round they said 🐉 who's the guy at your gym who says this every time? #bjj #nogi #rashguard #nogibjj #bjjhumor
- Design Ryu (navy). Both shots from ONE unused gym video (a man lying flat after a round; a man walking onto the mat back to camera); both people replaced with the same fictional description.
- Submits: day N-1 = still B and still A (2 submits, 20 min apart, both gated). Day N = clip B and clip A (2 submits), leaving 2 spare clip re-rolls that day.
- Fallback (never a new submit beyond the 4 a day, never a failed take): if one clip still fails after its re-roll, post nothing from F1 that day. A single PASSED complete clip may be posted alone only if it independently passes format-lab and ttevaluate as a one-shot post (e.g. passed clip B as rank-3-style "how it ended" is NOT enough on its own; it would need its own setup line and payoff), otherwise hold and post the backup slot (rank 2) the next day.
- Pass line (23 h, compared with earlier 23 h reads): full watch 25%+ and at least 2 shares or saves. Falsifier: two runs at or below 14% full watch.

### Rank 2 spec (backup slot): "the one nobody wants to roll with"
- One shot: a big, muscular fictional man standing at the mat edge with his back to the camera, rolling his shoulders (frame-0 motion). Text 1: "the guy nobody wants to roll with". At ~2.5 s he turns his head and shoulders to the camera with a sheepish, apologetic grin; his arms stay at his sides (no hand movement, to protect sleeves and print). Text 2: "says sorry every 5 seconds".
- Reversal: intimidating vs gentle, shown on his face. Stranger test: the archetype is readable without BJJ knowledge.
- 1 still + 1 clip (6 s), 2 spare. Pass: 1 s hold at or above 76% and 3+ comments. Falsifier: two runs with 0 comments.

## Branching test plan (TikTok, one post a day while Instagram stays paused)
- Run order: rank 1 (stills the day before, clips on the day), rank 2, rank 3, then rank 4 or 5 depending on which of ranks 1 to 3 holds best. The control (rank 6) runs at most once, as a same-age comparison.
- Each slot's backup is the next rank that fits the remaining submits and an already-passed base; a failed take is never posted.
- At each 23 h read: passes its line: rerun within 2 days on a new scene (still provisional). Two passes: becomes the main format for the next week, re-checked weekly. Misses but improves one metric: one revised run changing ONE structural element. Misses twice: dropped for now.
- Any platform integrity flag stops everything and is reported.

## Practical limitations
- One run per format, about 700 views each so far: verdicts are provisional; two runs before any decision; rates from 1 to 3 events are fragile.
- TikTok analytics can lag or misreport; a zero is not proof of suppression.
- In the one TikTok virality study we found (preprint, correlational), creator popularity was the strongest predictor; with 3 followers we start behind.
- Automation risk: Instagram has shown its own suspected-automated-behaviour warning (observed). TikTok's guideline wording found prohibits automation "to register or operate accounts in bulk"; whether single-account browser automation breaches either platform's terms is NOT established here. The observed Instagram warning is still the largest operational risk and Nic's decision.
- AI realism and motion: walking and dropping onto the mat are harder than sitting; both need per-frame QA and may cost the spare submits.
- No access from this workspace to watch competitor videos; examples gathered by Instinct are graded C unless the numbers come from the platform.
