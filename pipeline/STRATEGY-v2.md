# Shizen strategy v2.1 (19 Nov 2026, corrected after audit): format-first test plan
Built from pipeline/research/2026-11-19-virality-research.md and the format-lab skill (with checks adapted from four MIT community skill repos, see .claude/skills/format-lab/references/community-skills-audit.md). TikTok only (Instagram paused). Hands-off for Nic. Every post: unused real base, new identities, exact art, low motion, max 4 Flow submits a day, 20 min apart.

## What genuinely differs from the previous plan
- Previous plan: right goals, but every concept was produced as one near-still back, two text beats and a print reveal, because the 1 still + 1 clip budget quietly chose the format. P14 proved it.
- v2.1 designs the format first and requires, before any still: motion in the first second, an open loop paid off only at the end, a stranger test, and a structural difference from the last three posts on at least two axes (shot count, narrative shape, camera perspective, content job, length band, audio role). Wording, design, gym or CTA changes do not count.
- Every slot carries a backup concept and a hold rule; a failed take is never posted to fill a day.
- Results are read at the same ages (4 h, 23 h) as earlier reads where those exist. Lifetime totals of older posts are context, not a matched control.

## Context from our own posts (not a matched control)
TikTok, mostly lifetime totals at different ages: views 626 to 922 for 13 of 17 posts; where 23 h reads exist, average watch about 2.7 to 3.2 s on 5 to 8 s cuts, full watch 7 to 14% (Part 2: 4.26 s, 33%), 1 s hold 70 to 76%; comments 1 genuine in 17 posts; shares 0 to 1. Rates from about 700 views with 1 to 3 events are fragile.

## Formats

### F1. Flash-forward consequence (two shots, motion opener)
- Structure: shot B FIRST (0 to ~1.2 s): a man drops onto his back or face down on the mat after a round, motion in frame 0, text "how it ended". Then shot A (~1.2 s onward): earlier, the same fictional man walking onto the mat confidently, back to camera, print visible, text "how it started" and a BUT/THEREFORE turn. Last frame cuts back to him flat, looping into frame 1. 7 to 9 s.
- Differs from the template on: shot count, narrative shape, frame-0 motion, length band.
- Mechanism: before/after with the "after" flashed first (adapted from vyral formats.md); amusement with a clear consequence. Evidence: B (Part 2, a two-shot white-belt-humbled story: our best full watch, 33%, one run; our one genuine comment came from that joke); A for news-article sharing (Berger and Milkman 2012; not tested on video).
- Hypothesis (provisional): a two-shot consequence story holds people to the end more than one-shot puns.
- Budget: 2 stills + 2 clips = 4 submits. Both shots from ONE unused gym video; both identities replaced to the same fictional look.
- Fallback and hold: if still A or B fails twice, or either clip fails, do not post F1 that day. Use whatever passed: a passed still plus its passed clip can become the F6 single-shot post (walk-in or drop), only if it passes format-lab on its own; otherwise hold until the next day. Never post a failed take.
- Pass line (23 h): full watch clearly above our earlier 23 h reads (target 25%+) AND at least 2 shares or saves. Falsifier: two runs both at or below our earlier full-watch range (7 to 14%).

### F6. Walk-in reveal (single shot, motion opener) [new, outside the calm back shot]
- Structure: one shot that MOVES from frame 0: a man walks away from the camera onto the mat (or sits down hard onto the mat), the print revealed as he moves; text is a third-person micro-story in BUT/THEREFORE beats ("came to his first class / BUT forgot it was no gi day / THEREFORE wore this"). 6 to 8 s, last line hands back to the first.
- Differs on: frame-0 motion, narrative shape (three turning beats), camera relationship (subject moving through the frame).
- Mechanism: motion in the first second (adapted hadi viral-check Q4) plus an open loop. Evidence: C (rubric heuristic); B only that our still openings lost about a quarter of viewers by 1 s.
- Risk: walking motion is harder for the model than sitting (Part 1 walk-away shots were accepted before; low motion still applies: a few steps, arms relaxed).
- Budget: 1 still + 1 clip; 2 spare (one re-roll each).
- Pass: 1 s hold at or above 76% (Part 10's) AND average watch above 3.5 s. Falsifier: two runs with 1 s hold below 70%.

### F3. Third-person text story (8 s, one shot, 4 BUT/THEREFORE beats)
- Structure: a calm shot with four text beats about a fictional character, never "I": "his first comp" / "BUT he drew a guy in a white belt" / "THEREFORE he relaxed" / "turns out: black belt in judo". Last line written first; it loops back to "his first comp".
- Differs on: narrative shape (four turning beats), audio role (text-paced), length band.
- Mechanism: a sequence of open loops (adapted from artem storytelling). Evidence: preprint, correlational (on-screen text 67% vs 30% in a viral sample). No fabricated biography: it is a character, not the account's own story.
- Budget: 1 still + 1 clip at 8 s (12 credits). No looping or slowing footage to fake length.
- Pass: average watch above 5 s and full watch 15%+. Falsifier: two runs with average watch under 3.5 s.

### F4. Rule-check utility list (save and search), sourced claims only
- Structure: "IBJJF no gi rules your rash guard has to pass" as a numbered list over one shot, each line from the official IBJJF uniform page: 1 "skin tight, elastic", 2 "long enough to cover your torso to the shorts' waistband", 3 "black, white or black and white, with at least 10% of your belt colour (or 100% your belt colour)". Last beat: "check yours before you register". No claim that any Shizen design meets or fails these rules unless checked against the real garment spec, which we do not have; no construction or quality claims about Shizen.
- Differs on: content job (utility), narrative shape (list), caption (search phrase "ibjjf no gi rash guard rules").
- Source: https://ibjjf.com/uniform (fetched 19 Nov 2026; Claude re-checks wording on the day).
- Mechanism: practical utility (A for news-article sharing; untested on video); search queries reaching us (B).
- Budget: 1 still + 1 clip at 8 s (12 credits); edit 7.5 to 8 s. If only 6 s is offered, 6 s (10 credits) with three beats.
- Pass: 3+ saves or Search above 5% of traffic by day 3. Falsifier: zero saves in two runs.

### F2. Old-format CONTROL (labelled as such)
- Structure: the existing one-shot calm back plus text plus print payoff (P10, P13, P14 type). Kept only as a control so new formats are judged against a same-age read of the old one; at most one control in the test window.

### F5. Reply video (trigger only)
- TikTok reply-to-comment with the comment sticker when a genuine comment lands. Evidence: C.

## Branching test plan (TikTok, one post a day while Instagram stays paused)
- Day 1: F1 (backup: F6 from F1's passed half, or hold).
- Day 2: F6 (backup: F3 on the same base).
- Day 3: F4 (backup: F3).
- Day 4: F3 (backup: F2 control).
- Branch at each 23 h read: passes its line: rerun within 2 days on a new scene (still provisional). Two passes: becomes the main format for the next week, re-checked weekly. Misses but improves one metric: one revised run changing ONE structural element. Misses twice: dropped for now.
- Any platform integrity flag (warning, checkpoint, restriction) stops everything and is reported.

## Source plan
- F1 needs two moments of one unused gym video: a man walking onto the mat (back to camera), and a man dropping or lying flat after a round. F6 needs a man walking away or sitting down (motion). F3 and F4 need one unused solo back shot.

## Practical limitations
- One run per format, about 700 views each so far: verdicts are provisional; two runs before any decision; rates from 1 to 3 events are fragile.
- TikTok analytics can lag or misreport; a zero is not proof of suppression.
- In the one TikTok virality study we found (preprint, correlational), creator popularity was the strongest predictor; with 3 followers we start behind.
- Automation risk: Instagram has shown its own suspected-automated-behaviour warning (observed). TikTok's guideline wording found prohibits automation "to register or operate accounts in bulk"; whether single-account browser automation breaches either platform's terms is NOT established here. The observed Instagram warning is still the largest operational risk and Nic's decision.
- AI realism and motion: walking and dropping onto the mat are harder than sitting; both need per-frame QA and may cost the spare submits.
- No access from this workspace to watch competitor videos; examples gathered by Instinct are graded C unless the numbers come from the platform.
