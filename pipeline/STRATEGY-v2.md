# Shizen strategy v2 (19 Nov 2026): format-first test plan
Built from pipeline/research/2026-11-19-virality-research.md and the format-lab skill. TikTok only (Instagram paused). Hands-off for Nic. Every post: unused real base, new identities, exact art, low motion, max 4 Flow submits a day.

## What genuinely differs from the previous plan
- Previous plan: right goals, but every concept was produced as the same template (one near-still back, two text beats, design reveal), because the 1 still + 1 clip budget silently chose the format. P14 proved it: new gym, new line, same format.
- v2 designs the FORMAT first (job, mechanism, structure), then spends up to 4 submits on it. Each format below differs from the template on at least two structural axes (format-lab gate).
- v2 adds jobs we never tried: utility (save/search), identity naming (share/tag), and a long text-story (watch time).
- Decisions use age-matched 4 h and 23 h reads, per-1,000-view engagement, a pass line and a falsifier stated before posting.

## Baseline (P7 to P11, TikTok, 23 h or lifetime)
Views 676 to 922; average watch about 2.7 to 3.2 s (about 50 to 57% of 5.5 s); full watch 7 to 14%; 1 s hold 70 to 76%; likes 0.1 to 2.1 per 100 views; comments 0; shares 0 to 1.

## Formats (each with evidence, hypothesis, budget, pass line, falsifier)

### F1. Two-shot consequence story ("before / after")
- Structure: shot A (him confident, warming up or walking on) then shot B (him flattened, face down after the roll), text beat per shot, 7 to 9 s. Different from template on: shot count, narrative shape, length.
- Mechanism: open loop paid off by a visual consequence; amusement (share). Evidence: B (Part 2: 33% full watch, our best; first genuine comment came from "white belt humbled" humour); A (finishing is a strong signal; amusement raises sharing).
- Hypothesis: a story across two shots lifts full watch above 25% and shares above 2 per 1,000 views.
- Budget: 2 stills + 2 clips = 4 submits (one post per day; no spare). Source: two moments of ONE unused gym video (same man not required; both identities replaced to the same fictional look).
- Pass: full watch 25%+ AND (shares or saves 2+ per 1,000). Falsifier: two runs both under 15% full watch.

### F2. "The guy at every gym" identity card (second person)
- Structure: one shot, but second-person text that names a person type viewers know ("you know the guy who wears a new rash guard every week") plus a tag prompt; payoff is the print as "his latest one". 6 to 8 s. Different on: point of view, content job (identity), caption mechanism.
- Mechanism: recognition, then send it to the friend it describes (share/tag). Evidence: A- (second person more common in viral TikToks, correlational); B (P7 "tag your coach", our highest views, 922); C (relatable-contrast memes, CHILLOLOG).
- Hypothesis: naming a person type lifts shares and tagged comments above zero.
- Budget: 1 still + 1 clip; 2 spare.
- Pass: 3+ comments with tags OR shares 3+ per 1,000. Falsifier: two runs with 0 tags and 0 shares.

### F3. Text-story (8 s, one slow shot, 4 beats)
- Structure: one calm shot, 4 short first-person text beats with a twist at the end ("first comp" / "drew a guy in a white belt" / "turns out: black belt in judo" / "the rash guard did not save me"). Different on: length band, narrative shape, point of view, audio (text-paced).
- Mechanism: open loop and curiosity, held by reading; tests whether text holds people past 3 s. Evidence: A- (on-screen text 67% vs 30% in viral sample); C (storytime format widespread). Risk: lower completion on longer videos.
- Hypothesis: a 4-beat story holds people longer in absolute terms than our 2-beat puns.
- Budget: 1 still + 1 clip at 8 s (12 credits; no looping or slowed footage to fake length).
- Pass: average watch 5 s+ and full watch 15%+. Falsifier: average watch under 3.5 s twice.

### F4. Utility list (save and search)
- Structure: one shot of the back, text as a numbered list: "3 things to check before buying a no gi rash guard": 1 flatlock seams, 2 sleeve length, 3 the print won't crack (sublimated prints are dyed in, not printed on top). Last beat: "this one's sublimated". 8 to 10 s. Different on: content job (utility), narrative shape (list), caption (search phrase).
- Mechanism: practical utility drives sharing (A) and saves (C); search phrases match real queries reaching us (B).
- Honesty: only general, true buying tips; no claims about Shizen quality or availability. Claude checks each claim before posting.
- Budget: 1 still + 1 clip.
- Pass: saves 3+ per 1,000 OR search share above 5% by day 3. Falsifier: zero saves in two runs.

### F5. Reply video (whenever a genuine comment lands)
- Structure: TikTok reply-to-comment with the comment sticker; the answer acted out in one shot. Mechanism: commenter is featured, shares it; TikTok links the reply to the original. Evidence: C. Trigger-only.

## Branching test plan (TikTok, one post a day while Instagram stays paused)
- Day 1 to 4: F1, F2, F4, F3 (one each), from unused bases. Read each at 4 h and 23 h.
- Branch rule at each 23 h read:
  - Passes its line: run it again within 2 days with a new scene (n = 2). Two passes: it becomes the main format (3 of every 4 posts).
  - Misses but beats baseline on one metric: one revised run changing ONE structural element.
  - Misses everything twice: drop it (falsified for now).
- Day 5 to 8: second runs and the leading format. F5 any time a genuine comment arrives.
- Stop everything on any platform integrity flag (warning, checkpoint, restriction) and report.

## Source plan (fits F1 to F4)
- F1 needs two moments from one unused gym video: a man standing or walking with his back to the camera, and a man lying face down or sitting slumped after a round. Both are common in open-mat videos; neither needs a second person in frame.
- F2, F3, F4 need one unused solo back shot each (the Part 14 gate).
- Burned: art.of.roll, Coastal (both videos), Holy City.

## Practical limitations (honest)
- n = 1 per run and a ~700 test batch: only large differences mean anything; two runs per format before a verdict.
- TikTok analytics can lag or misreport (P12 shows 0 for 24 h+ with no explanation); a zero is not treated as proof of suppression.
- Creator popularity is the strongest predictor in the literature; with 3 followers we start behind, which is why shares, saves and search matter more than For You luck.
- The pipeline is automated through a browser agent. Instagram has now flagged suspected automation twice. Platform terms prohibit automated operation of accounts; continued automation risks the accounts. This is the largest risk to the whole project and is Nic's decision.
- AI realism: low motion only; faces and print checked every frame; one Flow account and 4 submits a day cap output at one post a day.
- No access from this workspace to watch competitor videos; examples gathered by Instinct are graded C unless the numbers come from the platform.
