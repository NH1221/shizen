## Rules so far
1. Every video must be understood with sound off in the first second (big text plus the strongest worn close-up).
2. One clear comment action ("<number> waitlist"), shown on screen as an example.
3. Show all 8 designs, worn by people, never artwork-only.
4. 11 to 13 s, fast VO, no slow intros.
