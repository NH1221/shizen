# Earn the back: QA rounds (for Nic's morning review)

The footage is `incoming/2026-10-30/F1-take1.mp4` in every version, untouched. Only the edit changes. Every cut was scored with `/ttevaluate`. A new standing rule 6 (cohesion) was added after Nic's note on v2.

## Voiceover: none, on purpose

- The format is a first-person POV in the middle of a roll. Nobody narrates while they're on someone's back, so any VO breaks the POV.
- Native BJJ POVs run on on-screen text plus the room's own sound.
- An AI voice is the first thing a slop-allergic viewer notices. A British voice doesn't change that.
- The muted-viewing risk is covered by the text. The breathing bed covers sound-on viewers.
